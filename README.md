CUSTOMER PRODUCT RESEARCH & COMPARISON GUIDE

ABOUT THIS GUIDE
This independent resource helps customers compare products across different retailers and industries. It applies to electronics, clothing, beauty products, home goods, accessories, and other consumer products.

PRODUCT EVALUATION CRITERIA
When comparing products, consider:
- Price and affordability
- Features and functionality
- Quality and durability
- Customer needs and preferences
- Long-term value
- Warranty and return policies

COMPARISON METHOD
1. Identify the customer's needs and budget.
2. Compare relevant product features.
3. Explain the advantages and disadvantages of each option.
4. Recommend the most suitable product based on the customer's priorities.
